01

Expresar emociones

4 expresiones
bottle upreprimir

Ejemplo 1Bottling frustration up usually makes the conversation harder.

Ejemplo 2He bottled his worries up until he could no longer concentrate.

let outdejar salir

Ejemplo 1She let out a long sigh after the meeting.

Ejemplo 2She let her frustration out in a long, honest conversation.

open upabrirse

Ejemplo 1He opened up about why the change worried him.

Ejemplo 2She opened up to a trusted friend after the move.

hold backcontener

Ejemplo 1I could not hold back my tears during the farewell.

Ejemplo 2He held his frustration back until the meeting was over.

Momentos difíciles

4 expresiones
break downderrumbarse emocionalmente

Ejemplo 1She broke down after weeks of pressure.

Ejemplo 2I nearly broke down when the final plan changed again.

lash outreaccionar con enojo

Ejemplo 1He lashed out when he felt cornered.

Ejemplo 2She regretted lashing out at someone who was trying to help.

Ejemplo 1Do not freak out; we still have time to fix it.

Ejemplo 2I freaked out when I could not find my passport.

Calmarse y regularse

4 expresiones

Ejemplo 2Slow breathing helped the group calm down before the presentation.

Ejemplo 1I need to slow down and focus on one task at a time.

Ejemplo 2She slowed her schedule down after several exhausting weeks.

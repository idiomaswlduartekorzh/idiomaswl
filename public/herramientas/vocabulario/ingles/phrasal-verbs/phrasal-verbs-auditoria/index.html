Lista por subtemas

Las etiquetas ↗ señalan expresiones que también aparecen en otros contextos o con otro significado. Ábrelas para seguir su trazabilidad.

01

Planificación

3 expresiones
map outdefinir el plan

Ejemplo 1We mapped out the audit timeline.

Ejemplo 2At the opening meeting, we mapped the key controls and owners out.

set outexponer o establecer

Ejemplo 1The memo sets out the scope of the review.

Ejemplo 2The engagement letter sets each party's responsibilities out clearly.

break downdesglosar

Ejemplo 1Break the expenses down by department.

Ejemplo 2The analyst broke the variance down by region and product line.

02

Evidencia

3 expresiones
back uprespaldar con evidencia

Ejemplo 1Invoices back up the reported amount.

Ejemplo 2Signed contracts back the revenue recognition decision up.

pull togetherreunir

Ejemplo 1Please pull together the supporting documents.

Ejemplo 2The controller pulled the reconciliations together before fieldwork began.

hand overentregar

Ejemplo 1Finance handed the records over to the auditors.

Ejemplo 2The payroll team handed the requested employee records over securely.

03

Revisión

4 expresiones
go throughrevisar cuidadosamente

Ejemplo 1We went through every sample item.

Ejemplo 2We went through the exception report line by line.

look intoinvestigar

Ejemplo 1The team looked into the unusual payment.

Ejemplo 2The auditors are looking into a sequence of duplicate payments.

check againstcomparar con

Ejemplo 1Check the figures against the bank statement.

Ejemplo 2I checked every sampled invoice against the purchase ledger.

tie outconciliar

Ejemplo 1The balance ties out to the general ledger.

Ejemplo 2After the adjustment, the subsidiary ledger tied out to the control account.

04

Hallazgos

3 expresiones
pick updetectar

Ejemplo 1The review picked up two control gaps.

Ejemplo 2Our automated test picked several unusual weekend transactions up.

point outseñalar

Ejemplo 1The auditor pointed out a missing approval.

Ejemplo 2The reviewer pointed the missing evidence out in her notes.

account forexplicar una diferencia

Ejemplo 1Can you account for this variance?

Ejemplo 2Management could not account for the difference between the two reports.

05

Reportes

3 expresiones
draw upredactar

Ejemplo 1We drew up the draft report.

Ejemplo 2The audit manager drew a concise findings summary up for the committee.

follow updar seguimiento

Ejemplo 1Internal audit followed up on the action plan.

Ejemplo 2We followed up on each overdue corrective action with its owner.

sign offaprobar formalmente

Ejemplo 1The partner signed off on the final report.

Ejemplo 2The engagement partner will sign the opinion off after the final review.

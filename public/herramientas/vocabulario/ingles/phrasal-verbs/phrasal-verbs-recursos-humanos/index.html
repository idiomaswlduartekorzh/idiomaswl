01

Reclutamiento

5 expresiones
draw upredactar

Ejemplo 1HR drew up a clearer job description.

Ejemplo 2The people team drew a competency framework up for the new role.

reach outcontactar

Ejemplo 1The recruiter reached out to three qualified candidates.

Ejemplo 2We reached out to former interns about the open positions.

screen outdescartar en una primera revisión

Ejemplo 1The application questions screen out candidates without the required license.

Ejemplo 2A work-authorization question screened several applications out.

narrow downreducir la selección

Ejemplo 1We narrowed the shortlist down to five people.

Ejemplo 2The panel narrowed the candidate pool down after the technical task.

call incitar

Ejemplo 1The hiring manager called two finalists in for a second interview.

Ejemplo 2HR called the strongest applicants in for an assessment day.

02

Entrevistas y oferta

5 expresiones
set upprogramar

Ejemplo 1The coordinator set up a panel interview for Tuesday.

Ejemplo 2I set a brief preparation call up with every interviewer.

follow updar seguimiento

Ejemplo 1Follow up with each candidate after the interview.

Ejemplo 2The recruiter followed up once the compensation package was approved.

take oncontratar o incorporar

Ejemplo 1The company took on twelve apprentices this year.

Ejemplo 2We are taking on a bilingual customer-support specialist.

turn downrechazar

Ejemplo 1She turned the offer down after reviewing the relocation terms.

Ejemplo 2Two candidates turned the offer down because of the start date.

sign onincorporarse o firmar contrato

Ejemplo 1Three graduate analysts signed on in September.

Ejemplo 2The new head of finance signed on after a three-month search.

03

Onboarding

4 expresiones

Ejemplo 2Please fill your emergency-contact details out before orientation.

Ejemplo 1The HR partner walked us through the benefits portal.

Ejemplo 2A payroll specialist walked each new hire through the first payslip.

Ejemplo 1A peer mentor helped the new designer settle in.

Ejemplo 2Regular check-ins helped the remote employees settle in.

Ejemplo 1The departing manager handed her team over to an interim lead.

Ejemplo 2Before leaving, she handed all active recruitment searches over.

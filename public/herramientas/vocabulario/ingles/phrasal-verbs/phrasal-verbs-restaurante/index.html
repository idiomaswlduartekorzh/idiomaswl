01

Reservas y llegada

5 expresiones
book upllenarse de reservas

Ejemplo 1The restaurant books up quickly on Fridays.

Ejemplo 2The private dining room is booked up for the whole weekend.

call aheadllamar con anticipación

Ejemplo 1Call ahead if you need a table for eight.

Ejemplo 2We called ahead to ask whether the kitchen could handle a nut allergy.

check inconfirmar la llegada

Ejemplo 1We checked in with the host at the entrance.

Ejemplo 2Please check in with the host once your whole party has arrived.

show uppresentarse o llegar

Ejemplo 1Two guests showed up late.

Ejemplo 2If a guest does not show up, we hold the table for fifteen minutes.

sit downsentarse

Ejemplo 1The server invited us to sit down.

Ejemplo 2We sat down near the window and looked through the menu.

02

Cocina y preparación

5 expresiones
chop upcortar en trozos

Ejemplo 1Chop up the onions before heating the pan.

Ejemplo 2The prep cook chopped the parsley up just before service.

cut upcortar en porciones

Ejemplo 1The chef cut up the chicken for the stew.

Ejemplo 2Could you cut the limes up into thin wedges?

mix inincorporar un ingrediente

Ejemplo 1Mix in the herbs at the end.

Ejemplo 2Slowly mix in the cream so the sauce stays smooth.

roll outestirar una masa

Ejemplo 1Roll out the dough on a floured surface.

Ejemplo 2The pastry chef rolled the dough out until it was paper-thin.

warm upcalentar

Ejemplo 1Warm the sauce up before service.

Ejemplo 2We warmed the bread up while the guests chose their drinks.

03

Cocción

4 expresiones

Ejemplo 1Turn down the heat before the pot boils over.

Ejemplo 2The stock boiled over while the cook was plating another dish.

cook throughcocinar completamente

Ejemplo 1Make sure the chicken cooks through.

Ejemplo 2Leave the fish in the oven until it has cooked through.

fry upfreír una preparación

Ejemplo 1The cook fried up some vegetables.

Ejemplo 2They fried up a fresh batch of plantain chips for our table.

turn downbajar la intensidad

Ejemplo 1Turn the heat down once the soup starts boiling.

Ejemplo 2The chef turned down the flame to keep the sauce from burning.

4 expresiones

Ejemplo 2Our server brought the appetizers out as soon as we sat down.

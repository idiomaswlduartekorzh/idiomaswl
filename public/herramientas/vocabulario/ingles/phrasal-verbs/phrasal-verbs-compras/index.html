01

Buscar y comparar

4 expresiones
look aroundrecorrer mirando

Ejemplo 1We looked around the market before choosing a stall.

Ejemplo 2We looked around several furniture shops before ordering the chairs.

look forbuscar

Ejemplo 1I am looking for a waterproof jacket.

Ejemplo 2She is looking for a reusable bottle that fits her bag.

try onprobarse

Ejemplo 1She tried the blue shoes on in the fitting room.

Ejemplo 2Can I try this jacket on in a larger size?

check outexaminar

Ejemplo 1Check out the new selection of local coffee.

Ejemplo 2We checked the handmade ceramics out near the entrance.

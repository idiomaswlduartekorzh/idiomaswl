# Goethe A2 Listening author

Produce 20 original scored items in four five-item parts. Teil 1 has five short messages played twice. Teil 2 is one continuous conversation matched to nine functional image concepts and played once; it must include a guide question, a stage/day table, one worked example 0 that consumes one image, five unique scored answers and exactly three unused distractors. Teil 3 has five short conversations with three visual-only options each and is played once. Teil 4 is one radio-style interview with one worked example 0 plus five Ja/Nein statements and is played twice. Keep script density aligned with the observed adult references: 45–70 words per Teil 1/3 clip, 220–260 words for Teil 2 and 190–230 for Teil 4. Provide scripts, pauses, speaker intents, answer evidence, distractor rationales and a master-track assembly plan. Do not generate audio, spend credits, expose transcripts in candidate UI or reuse official wording. Write only the isolated listening artifact.

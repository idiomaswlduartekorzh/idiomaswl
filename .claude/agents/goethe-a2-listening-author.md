# Goethe A2 Listening author

Produce 20 original scored items in four five-item parts. Teil 1 has five short messages played twice; Teil 2 is one continuous conversation matched to nine functional image concepts and played once; Teil 3 has five short conversations with three visual options each and is played once; Teil 4 is one radio-style interview with five Ja/Nein statements and is played twice. Provide scripts, pauses, speaker intents, answer evidence, distractor rationales and a master-track assembly plan. Do not generate audio, spend credits, expose transcripts in candidate UI or reuse official wording. Write only the isolated listening artifact.

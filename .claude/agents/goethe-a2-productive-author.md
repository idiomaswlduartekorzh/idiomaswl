# Goethe A2 productive-skills author

Create Schreiben Teil 1 as an informal 20–30 word message and Teil 2 as a context-appropriate semi-official 30–40 word email; each must require exactly three independently scorable communicative functions. Create Sprechen Teil 1 with four keyword cards per candidate, Teil 2 with distinct personal topics plus four cues and follow-ups, and Teil 3 with complementary constraint cards that force proposing, reacting and reaching agreement. Do not provide a model answer that can be copied during the attempt. Write only the isolated productive artifact.

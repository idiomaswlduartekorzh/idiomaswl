## ROLE A — The house is paid, in your name

> **Informal.** Friends, first names, you can cut in. Tomorrow, same car. **Kevin starts.** 7 turns · 6 minutes.
> **Your screen only.** Don't show it, don't read from it.

**Where you are** · Friday, the parking lot, 7:20 p.m. Kevin comes down with his bag.

**You want** · Six people, two cars, tomorrow at 8:00. From Kevin: the **100,000**, or a date in the group tonight · his **car at 8:00**, his gas · **name + ID before 7:00 a.m.** for anyone new.

**Your own rule** · Nothing decided until you know who those two are and why they matter to him.

**You can't**
1. Put money in again. You paid 600,000; his part is missing. You *can* pay it — not for a stranger.
2. Get anyone in the back way. No name + ID on the list, no entry; only doña Nubia changes it.
3. Drop Hernán for a spot. He and his car go together: four seats leave with him.

**Only you know**
- **Hernán's car: at the shop since 6:00 p.m.**, a noise in the engine; **the mechanic calls back at 7:00 a.m.** Telling him hands him an argument; not telling him, you bargain blind.
- **The sixth was yours, Wednesday** — Hernán, car and gas, written in the group, never said to Kevin's face.
- **Sebastián you know** — last year's trip. **Andrea you've never seen.**

**If you walk away with nothing** · Six people for five seats tomorrow, and the five are his. Nobody has paid you.

## ROLE B — Two more people, and their money in your pocket

> **Informal.** Friends, first names, you can cut in. Tomorrow, same car. **You start.** 7 turns · 6 minutes.
> **Your screen only.** Don't show it, don't read from it.

**Where you are** · Friday, the parking lot, 7:20 p.m. Your bag goes in the trunk.

**You want** · Sebastián and Andrea on the trip — both, or whichever one is possible — and **tonight**, a name and ID for doña Nubia. And **Tuesday the 25th** for the 100,000.

**And a reason you can repeat** · **Andrea gets here in twenty minutes.** If nobody goes, you tell her tonight — and *"Valentina said no"* won't hold.

**You can't**
1. Pay the 100,000 today. The 200,000 on you are Sebastián's and Andrea's.
2. Keep that money past tonight. Whoever doesn't go gets it back tonight.
3. Walk them in yourself. The guard turns back anyone not on the list, name and ID.

**Only you know**
- **Tuesday: 100,000 collected from each of them.** If nobody goes: 200,000 back tonight, two people let down.
- **On Tuesday the group was five**, and you said yes to two. The sixth came after; nobody told you.
- **Three lines of your table she doesn't have:** Andrea's twenty minutes, Sebastián's shift, the hammock. Only open questions get them out.

**If you walk away with nothing** · 200,000 out of your pocket tonight, two people to face, a debt with no date.

| Leaving | Saturday, 8:00 a.m., from the parking lot |

## Both screens — how it ends

**It ends when Kevin dictates out loud the message for the WhatsApp group, Valentina confirms it or fixes one thing in it, and Valentina sends it.** Before he dictates it, the two of you have to have answered out loud:

1. What happens tomorrow at 8:00 a.m., and what it depends on: which cars leave, and who rides where.
2. Who calls or writes to who tonight, and what for: Andrea, Sebastián, doña Nubia.
3. What is still open, at what time the two of you talk again tomorrow, and what you do if there is no answer by 7:00 a.m.

**The dictated message has to say all three.** When Valentina sends it, it's over.

## After — both screens, in Spanish

1. Este escenario **no se cierra hoy**, y eso estaba decidido antes de que hablaran: el administrador contesta a las 7:00 a. m. y el mecánico también. Salir con un mensaje escrito, una hora y un plan B era lo que había que conseguir. Lo que sí se medía es otra cosa: si se quejaron sin acusar, y si dijeron que no sin romper nada.
2. ¿En qué momento dijo cada uno **por qué** le importaba? ¿Antes o después de discutir las cifras? ¿Cambió algo en la conversación cuando se dijo?
3. El lío lo hicieron entre los dos: uno le dijo que sí a dos personas para un puesto, la otra metió al sexto al día siguiente sin preguntar. ¿Salió eso en la conversación, o cada uno defendió solo su mitad?

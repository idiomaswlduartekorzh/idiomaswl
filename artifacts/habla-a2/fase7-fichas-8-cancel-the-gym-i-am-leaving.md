## ROLE A — You're leaving, and the plan is still running

> **Counter, formal.** *Please*, *sorry*, *thank you*. Shouting buys nothing: she signs nothing. **You start.** 8 turns · 7 minutes.
> **Your screen only.** Don't show it, don't read from it.

**Where you are** · Tuesday, gym reception in Cabecera, 6:40 p.m. Four people behind you. Straight from work.

**You want** · Plan canceled today · no charge on September 5 · out of here in twenty minutes.

**Not about money** · The 92,000: payable. Two months of a gym you can't use: no.

**You can't**
1. Leave the embassy email, or a photo. On your screen, yes. In her hands, no.
2. Come back in office hours. You work 7:00–6:00. One gap: **Thursday the 27th, 12:00–1:00**.
3. Ask for money back, or argue the price.

**A permission, not a rule** · Your destination and your reason: nobody's business. Dates are enough.

**Only you know**
- **Card expires August 31.** New one: 8 working days, to an address you won't be at. The September 5 charge **bounces whatever you do**. Said early, it sounds like a plan to stop paying. Yours to time.
- **Wilmer**, July 13, spoken, never written: *leaves the country → they cancel it*. Gone from here. The 3-month minimum **was** written, and you signed it. No proof.
- **Ticket bought, in your name.** August 30. **One way.**
- **Yurany**, from work, asked about this gym two weeks ago; the price stopped her. Back Wednesday night.
- **No return date.** Passport: 10 to 15 working days after the appointment.

**If you walk away with nothing** · Thursday is your last day here. You fly with the plan running, and no counter to come back to.

## ROLE B — The one line you can't cross

> **Counter, formal.** *Please*, *sorry*, *thank you* — also for the no. **She starts.** 8 turns · 7 minutes.
> **Your screen only.** Don't show it, don't read from it.

**Where you are** · Tuesday, gym reception in Cabecera, 6:40 p.m. Four in line. At the counter: a member, not here to train. Her plan: sold by Wilmer, who left.

**You want** · To serve her without crossing the line. Four things:
1. **Proof that works** — see Facts.
2. **The return date.**
3. **Signature + ID in the visit log.** No log, no case — complaints land on you.
4. **The reason, in her words**, for the form.

**You can't**
1. Cancel a plan inside its minimum term. That's retention's, in writing. Not yours.
2. File a change with no proof with both dates. *(Opening a case isn't filing a change — that you can do today.)*
3. Ask where she's going, what for, or what if they say no. Allowed: **when she comes back**.

**Only you know**
- **Opening the case today fixes the date.** Édison then resolves with the case date. No number: cut-off missed, plan renewed, **92,000 more**. It's all you have today.
- **Transfers exist.** Not on the board, not your first offer: paperwork, and a line. Unsaid, she never knows.
- **A freeze is not a cancellation.** She'll think it is.
- **Written warning last month** for a freeze filed with no proof — for helping too much. It costs to say it.
- **Wilmer was fired for promising things to sell**, and not alone.

**If you walk away with nothing** · No signature, no reason for the box, and the next complaint has your name.

|---|---|
| Now | Tuesday, August 25, 6:40 p.m. · four people in line |
| Her plan | 12 months · **92,000 pesos a month** · signed **July 13** · **3-month minimum**, ends **October 13** · charged the **5th** |
| Proof that works | name + **date out and date back**: a return ticket · a letter from work with both dates · a booking in her name with both dates |
| Proof that doesn't | an email with no dates · **a one-way ticket** |
| A freeze | up to **60 days**, with proof · stops the charge · **moves the 3 months to the end** |
| A transfer | **30,000 pesos** *(thirty thousand)* · both here, with ID · only for someone who has never been a member |
| Open a case today | her signature + her ID + her reason · **no proof needed** · keeps **today's date** · without it she misses the cut-off and the plan renews: **one more month, 92,000** |
| File a change | needs proof with **both dates** · Édison signs it · **not today** |
| Cut-off | **Thursday the 27th, 6:00 p.m.** |
| Édison (retention) | 2nd floor · Wednesday to Friday, **9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.** · he left at 5:00 and doesn't answer at night |

|---|---|---|
| a contract | the paper both sides sign, with the rules on it | hers says three months, and she signed it |
| minimum term | the shortest time a member has to stay after signing | July 13 to October 13 — the line you can't cross |
| to freeze a membership | to stop the payments for some weeks and keep the plan | road one, and she hears "cancel" unless you explain it |
| to transfer a plan | to put a plan in another person's name | road two, the one she doesn't know exists |
| proof | a paper that shows something is true | you can't file anything without it |

| to authorize | to say officially that a thing can be done | Édison does, second floor. You don't |
| cut-off | the last hour of the month for a change to count in that month | Thursday, 6:00 p.m. |
| to open a case | to write a visit down and give it a number and a date | the only thing you can hand her today |

## The card — Milena's screen only · opens after **GLOBAL turn 3**

> **Global turn 3 = the third turn of the conversation**, not your third one. She opens here, so turn 3 is her second one and you play the card on turn 4. She never sees this screen.

> **Message from Édison · 6:52 p.m.** — he finally answers the one you sent at the start.

| Cancellations for travel | **not authorized · not one** |
|---|---|
| A freeze | **yes** · up to 60 days · **only with the two dates** |
| If the charge on the 5th bounces | the system sends it to **collections on its own, on the 12th** — after that nobody here handles it |
| Where he is | **not Thursday — inventory count** · **Wednesday, 9 to 5, second floor** |

> **Opening it early buys you nothing.** It brings one thing you have to tell her anyway — the 12th — and a correction to an office day you will already have said out loud. She only learns what you tell her. And from here on, "let me check" is gone: the no is yours to hold.

## Both screens — how it ends

**It ends when Tatiana signs the visit log, Milena says the case number and the date out loud, and Tatiana repeats them back to check she copied them right.** Before she signs, the two of you have to have answered out loud:

1. **What gets written today and what doesn't.** Tatiana says it, in her own words.
2. **Which road she tries first, and what she has to bring.** One road, not two.
3. **Who with, where and at what time — and what happens on September 5 and on the 12th if she does nothing.**

When the number is said, written down and repeated, it's over.

## After — both screens, in Spanish

1. **Hoy nadie consigue la cancelación**, y eso estaba decidido por el escenario antes de que hablaran: no depende de cómo lo hicieron y no es fallar el ejercicio. Lo que se medía es otra cosa: si Tatiana salió con más de lo que traía, y si Milena dijo que no **y** dijo qué sí.
2. ¿Cuáles de los «no» de Milena eran suyos y cuáles eran de otro? Al oírlos, ¿se notaba la diferencia?
3. Tatiana sabía desde el primer turno que el cobro del 5 iba a rebotar. ¿Lo contó? ¿En qué turno? ¿Le sirvió de algo callárselo?

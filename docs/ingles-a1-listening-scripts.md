# Inglés A1 — Guiones de escucha

20 monólogos para la práctica de escucha A1. Cada texto está pensado para durar
aproximadamente 30 segundos a un ritmo natural y claro de 95–105 palabras por
minuto. Grabar una sola voz por pista, con pausas naturales entre oraciones y
sin música ni efectos.

## 01. Hello, I’m Emma

**Foco:** presentarse · `to be` · países y profesiones  
**Palabras clave:** name, from, live, student, class

Hello. My name is Emma Brown, and I am twenty years old. I am from London, but I live in a small town near the city. I am a student, and I study art at college. My English class is on Tuesday and Thursday. I am happy to meet new people and learn new things.

## 02. My Family

**Foco:** familia · posesivos · `to be`  
**Palabras clave:** parents, brother, sister, work, funny

Hi. This is my family. My parents are Tom and Sarah. My father is a bus driver, and my mother is a nurse. I have one brother and one sister. My brother is twelve, and my sister is sixteen. We live in the same house. My family is small, but we are very happy together.

## 03. My Bedroom

**Foco:** `there is / there are` · preposiciones de lugar  
**Palabras clave:** bedroom, bed, desk, window, next to

My bedroom is small, but it is comfortable. There is a bed next to the window. On the bed, there are two blue pillows. My desk is in front of the window, and my books are on the desk. There is a small lamp beside my computer. My clothes are in a white cupboard near the door.

## 04. Our House

**Foco:** habitaciones de la casa · `there is / there are`  
**Palabras clave:** kitchen, living room, garden, upstairs, downstairs

I live in a house with my family. Downstairs, there is a kitchen, a living room, and a small bathroom. The kitchen is my favorite room because we eat there together. Upstairs, there are three bedrooms. My parents have one room, my sister has one room, and I have my room. Behind the house, there is a lovely garden.

## 05. My Town

**Foco:** ciudad · preposiciones de lugar · lugares cotidianos  
**Palabras clave:** town, supermarket, bank, park, opposite

My town is small and quiet. There is a supermarket near my house, so my mother and I walk there on Saturday. The bank is opposite the supermarket. There is also a park behind the library. I like the park because there are many trees and a little lake. In summer, people sit there and eat ice cream.

## 06. My Morning Routine

**Foco:** presente simple · horas · rutina  
**Palabras clave:** wake up, shower, breakfast, leave, bus

On weekdays, I wake up at half past six. First, I have a shower and get dressed. Then I make breakfast. I usually eat bread, fruit, and eggs, and I drink tea. At quarter past seven, I leave home and walk to the bus stop. My bus arrives at twenty past seven. I get to college at eight o’clock.

## 07. A Day at Work

**Foco:** presente simple · profesiones · horarios  
**Palabras clave:** office, emails, lunch, customer, finish

I work in a small office in the city. I start work at nine o’clock every morning. First, I check my emails and talk to my team. At twelve thirty, I have lunch with two friends. In the afternoon, I answer phone calls and help customers. I usually finish work at five. Then I take the train home.

## 08. My Week

**Foco:** días de la semana · adverbios de frecuencia  
**Palabras clave:** usually, sometimes, always, Tuesday, Friday

My week is very busy. On Monday, I go to my English class after work. On Tuesday, I usually visit my grandmother. I always play football with my friends on Wednesday evening. Thursday is a quiet day at home. On Friday, I sometimes go to the cinema with my sister. At the weekend, I relax and see my family.

## 09. Saturday Morning

**Foco:** actividades de fin de semana · presente simple  
**Palabras clave:** Saturday, market, coffee, clean, afternoon

Saturday is my favorite day. I do not get up early, so I wake up at eight thirty. Then I go to the market with my father. We buy fruit, vegetables, bread, and flowers. After that, we have coffee in a small café. In the afternoon, I clean my room and listen to music. In the evening, I watch a film.

## 10. My Class Schedule

**Foco:** hora · preposiciones de tiempo · asignaturas  
**Palabras clave:** starts, ends, at, on, subject

My classes start at nine o’clock every day. On Monday, I have English and history. My English class is in room twelve. On Tuesday, I study science in the morning and art in the afternoon. I have lunch at one o’clock with my classmates. Classes end at four. After class, I often study in the library for one hour.

## 11. My Shopping List

**Foco:** comida · cantidades simples · `have got`  
**Palabras clave:** shopping list, milk, apples, bottle, need

Today I am going to the supermarket because we have not got much food at home. I need a bottle of milk, a loaf of bread, and six eggs. I also want some apples and bananas for breakfast. My brother likes orange juice, so I buy a carton for him. We have got pasta, but we have not got any tomatoes.

## 12. My Favorite Lunch

**Foco:** gustos · comida · presente simple  
**Palabras clave:** favorite, usually, rice, chicken, delicious

My favorite lunch is chicken with rice and vegetables. I usually eat it on Sunday when my family is at home. My mother cooks the chicken in the oven, and my father makes a salad. We put tomatoes, carrots, and lettuce in the salad. I drink water with my lunch. The food is simple, healthy, and delicious.

## 13. What I Wear to School

**Foco:** ropa · colores · presente simple  
**Palabras clave:** wear, trousers, shirt, shoes, weather

I wear simple clothes to school. I usually wear black trousers and a white shirt. My shoes are brown, and my bag is blue. When the weather is cold, I wear a grey jumper and a warm coat. In summer, I do not wear my coat. I like comfortable clothes because I walk and take the bus every day.

## 14. The Weather Today

**Foco:** clima · `to be` · ropa  
**Palabras clave:** sunny, cloudy, windy, umbrella, jacket

Today the weather is strange. In the morning, it is cold and cloudy. At midday, the sun is out, and it is warm. Now it is windy, and there are dark clouds in the sky. I have got an umbrella in my bag because it can rain later. I am wearing a jacket, but I do not need it now.

## 15. My Dog, Max

**Foco:** mascotas · posesivos · presente simple  
**Palabras clave:** dog, friendly, walk, ball, hungry

I have got a dog called Max. He is small, brown, and very friendly. Max is five years old. Every morning, he waits by the door because he wants to go for a walk. He loves playing with his red ball in the park. At home, he sleeps under the table. Max is always hungry, but he only eats dog food.

## 16. What Is Happening in the Park?

**Foco:** presente continuo · acciones ahora  
**Palabras clave:** running, sitting, reading, playing, sleeping

I am sitting in the park now. It is a lovely afternoon. A little boy is running near the trees, and his mother is watching him. Two girls are playing with a ball. An old man is reading a newspaper on a bench. Near the lake, some ducks are swimming. My dog is sleeping next to my feet.

## 17. Things I Can Do

**Foco:** `can / can’t` · habilidades  
**Palabras clave:** can, cannot, swim, cook, guitar

I can do many useful things. I can cook simple meals, make coffee, and use a computer. I can also swim, but I cannot swim very fast. My sister can play the guitar, but I cannot play an instrument. I can speak a little French and English. I cannot drive a car yet, but I can ride a bike.

## 18. My Plan for Saturday

**Foco:** planes cercanos · presente simple con tiempo futuro  
**Palabras clave:** plan, Saturday, visit, meet, evening

This Saturday is going to be busy. In the morning, I am visiting my aunt and uncle. Their house is near the river, and we are having breakfast together. At two o’clock, I am meeting my friend Leo in town. We are going to look at some new books. In the evening, my family is having dinner at my grandmother’s house.

## 19. How to Get to the Library

**Foco:** direcciones · imperativos · preposiciones  
**Palabras clave:** go straight, turn left, corner, next to, opposite

The library is easy to find. Start at the bus station and go straight for two minutes. Then turn left at the bank. Walk past the supermarket and the post office. The library is on the corner, next to a small café. It is opposite the park. The entrance is on Green Street, and it opens at nine o’clock.

## 20. My Ideal Day

**Foco:** repaso A1: rutina, gustos, clima, `can`, presente continuo  
**Palabras clave:** ideal, sunny, breakfast, friends, relax

My ideal day is a sunny Saturday in spring. I wake up late and have a big breakfast with my family. Then I meet my friends in the park. We can play football, talk, and eat sandwiches. In the afternoon, I read a book or listen to music at home. In the evening, we are all together, watching a funny film.

## Pauta de producción

- Mantener una sola voz por audio; no convertir ningún texto en diálogo.
- Ritmo recomendado: 95–105 palabras por minuto; vocalización clara, no teatral.
- Hacer una pausa breve después de cada oración, sin añadir silencios largos.
- Entregar idealmente MP3 a 44.1 kHz o WAV; conservar exactamente el texto grabado.
- Si se cambia una palabra durante la grabación, anotarla: el texto y el audio deben coincidir para crear la transcripción sincronizada.

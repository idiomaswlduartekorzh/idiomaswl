## Editorial quality gate

Every A2 lesson must include:

- at least 650 explanatory words;
- at least six explanatory tables and 30 total table rows;
- at least eight examples in the visual map;
- at least eight guided decisions;
- at least 40 practice interactions;
- all five supported exercise types across at least six progressive stages.

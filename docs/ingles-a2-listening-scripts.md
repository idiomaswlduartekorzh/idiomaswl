# Inglés A2 — Guiones de escucha: *The Corner Project*

## Concepto de la temporada

Maya encuentra un pequeño local vacío al lado de una biblioteca y decide abrir un
café comunitario de fin de semana. Cada audio es un monólogo de Maya, contado como
una actualización breve para sus seguidores. La historia permite trabajar una
secuencia real: descubrir un lugar, resolver problemas, pedir ayuda, planear,
abrir y pensar en el futuro.

Los 15 episodios siguen exactamente la progresión de Gramática Inglés A2 de
Idiomas WeLearn. El nivel se mantiene A2 estricto: frases conectadas, experiencias
personales y planes sencillos; sin giros idiomáticos densos ni vocabulario técnico
innecesario.

**Producción:** una sola voz por audio, tono cercano y claro, aproximadamente
65–80 palabras por episodio. Ritmo recomendado: 105–115 palabras por minuto.

---

## 01. The Empty Shop

**Foco A2:** Past Simple — verbos regulares  
**Gancho:** “I found something strange after work.”

Yesterday, I walked home through my neighborhood and noticed an empty shop next to the library. The windows looked dirty, but the place had beautiful old tiles and a small kitchen. I stopped, looked inside, and imagined a friendly café there. Later, I called the owner and visited the shop. I really liked it, so I rented it for three weekends. I took photos of every room and made a simple list of ideas at home. Now I have a big project, and I cannot stop thinking about it.

## 02. My Grandfather’s Key

**Foco A2:** Past Simple — verbos irregulares  
**Gancho:** “This old key changed my plan.”

This morning, my grandfather gave me an old key from his first shop. He said that he opened a small bakery many years ago, but he never kept the key. He worked there with my grandmother and sold bread before sunrise. I took the key to the empty café and put it on a shelf. Then I saw an idea: the café can be a place where people bring old objects and tell their stories. My grandfather made me feel brave and reminded me that small places can be important.

## 03. The First Problem

**Foco A2:** Past Simple — negativo y preguntas con *did*  
**Gancho:** “I didn’t expect this on day one.”

I didn’t clean the shop yesterday because the water didn’t work. At first, I felt worried. Did the owner know about the problem? Did someone forget to pay the water bill? I called her, and she explained that the pipes were old. A plumber visited this morning and fixed them. He showed me the broken part and explained the problem very simply. I didn’t lose the day after all. I learned that every project has a surprise, so I need to stay calm.

## 04. Rainy Saturday

**Foco A2:** Past Continuous  
**Gancho:** “Everything was going well, and then it started raining.”

Last Saturday, my friends and I were painting the walls when the rain started. My brother was carrying boxes inside, and my neighbor was cleaning the front window. We were all working quietly when water came under the door. For ten minutes, nobody was painting. We were moving chairs and towels instead. One friend was laughing because she was standing on a wet chair. Luckily, the rain stopped, and we finished the blue wall before dinner.

## 05. A Better Name

**Foco A2:** Comparatives  
**Gancho:** “The first name was too boring.”

My first idea was to call the place Maya’s Café, but that name was less interesting than I wanted. My friend Leo suggested The Corner Project. It sounded more creative and more connected to the neighborhood. The new sign is also brighter than the old one. It has yellow letters and a small drawing of a key. A local artist painted it more carefully than I expected. I think simple ideas are sometimes better than expensive ones, especially when they tell a real story.

## 06. The Best Table

**Foco A2:** Superlatives  
**Gancho:** “This table has the best story in the café.”

Today, I found the oldest table in a second-hand market. It is not the cleanest table, and it is definitely not the most modern one, but it is perfect. The seller told me that it came from a family restaurant. I put it near the window because that is the brightest place in the café. It is also the quietest corner in the morning. I hope it becomes the most popular table for people who want to read, talk, or write their own stories.

## 07. Opening Weekend

**Foco A2:** Future with *going to*  
**Gancho:** “We are finally going to open.”

Next weekend, we are going to open The Corner Project for the first time. I am going to make coffee, tea, and simple sandwiches. Leo is going to play quiet music in the afternoon, and my grandfather is going to bring some old photos for the story wall. We are not going to sell expensive food. My sister is going to help people write small notes about their favorite places. We are going to create a warm place where people can stay for a few minutes and feel part of the neighborhood.

## 08. A Promise to the Library

**Foco A2:** Future with *will*  
**Gancho:** “I made a promise I have to keep.”

The librarian visited today and gave me a great idea. She said the café could help people find new books. I said, “I will put a small book list on every table.” I will also give free tea to anyone who brings a book to share. I will ask the librarian to choose one easy book every week. It will not make a lot of money, but it will make the café special. I think people will enjoy this small surprise and perhaps meet someone new through a book.

## 09. What We Have Already Done

**Foco A2:** Present Perfect  
**Gancho:** “I can’t believe how far we have come.”

We have finished the painting, and we have cleaned every shelf. I have learned how to use the coffee machine, although I have made a few terrible coffees too. My friends have brought chairs, cups, and plants from their homes. We have not opened the doors yet, but the café already feels alive. My grandfather has added his key to the story wall. I have never worked on something this personal before, and I am proud of it.

## 10. The Menu Test

**Foco A2:** Countable / uncountable nouns — *some / any*  
**Gancho:** “I made too much soup and not enough cake.”

Today, I tested the first menu. We have some bread, some cheese, and a lot of fresh fruit. We do not have any meat because I want the menu to stay simple. I made some vegetable soup, but I did not make any cake. My brother asked for a banana cake, so I bought some bananas. We also need some milk and a little sugar for coffee. We need a few more ingredients before opening day, but the kitchen already smells great.

## 11. The Budget Jar

**Foco A2:** Quantifiers — *much, many, a lot of, a few, a little*  
**Gancho:** “This jar is teaching me about money.”

I do not have much money left, but I have a lot of ideas. We need many small things: cups, napkins, labels, and lights. Luckily, I only need a little paint for the outside sign. A few neighbors have offered chairs, and one person gave us a lot of old books. We have a few clean cups, but we do not have many plates yet. I am learning that you do not need much money when many people want to help.

## 12. Rules for Opening Day

**Foco A2:** Obligation — *should, must, have to*  
**Gancho:** “Opening a café has more rules than I thought.”

Before we open, I have to check the kitchen one more time. We must keep the floor dry, and we must wash our hands before making food. My friends should arrive early because the first morning may be busy. We also have to keep the door clear for people with bags or strollers. I think we should smile, even if something goes wrong. People do not expect a perfect café. They just want to feel welcome and safe.

## 13. If It Rains Again

**Foco A2:** First Conditional  
**Gancho:** “I have a plan for the weather this time.”

The weather forecast says it may rain on opening day. If it rains, we will move the small tables inside. If too many people arrive, my brother will make the drinks while I serve the food. If the café feels too quiet, Leo will play music near the window. If someone brings an old object, my grandfather will help them add it to the story wall. I cannot control everything, but if we stay calm, people will remember the good feeling of the place.

## 14. Why I Started

**Foco A2:** Gerunds and infinitives  
**Gancho:** “I didn’t start this project to become famous.”

People have asked me why I decided to open a café. I love meeting new people, and I enjoy listening to their stories. At first, I wanted to create a quiet place for readers. Then I began to imagine neighbors sharing ideas together. I also like making simple food for people who are busy or tired. I do not expect to become rich. I just hope to keep learning, to meet interesting people, and to give this empty corner a new life.

## 15. The First Real Morning

**Foco A2:** Adverbs of manner  
**Gancho:** “The doors opened, and everything changed.”

This morning, I opened the café slowly and carefully. The first customer walked in quietly and looked at the old table. Then two students came in happily because they saw the book list. My grandfather spoke warmly to a woman near the window, and she added an old photo to the story wall. We worked quickly, but we listened carefully to every person. At the end of the day, I closed the door gently and thought: this is only the beginning.

---

## 16. The First Review

**Foco A2:** Present Perfect with *ever / never*  
**Gancho:** “Someone has written about us online.”

This afternoon, Leo showed me a short review on the neighborhood website. A woman wrote that she has never visited a café with a story wall before. She has tried our vegetable soup, and she has already brought her daughter to choose a book. I have read the review three times because it feels unreal. We have not received many reviews yet, but this one is kind and honest. It says our café feels small, warm, and different from the big places in town. I have never felt so grateful for one paragraph.

## 17. The Missing Key

**Foco A2:** Past Simple and Past Continuous — story sequence  
**Gancho:** “The key disappeared during our busiest hour.”

Yesterday afternoon, the café was getting busy when I noticed that my grandfather’s old key was missing from the story wall. Leo was making drinks, my sister was helping two children, and I was carrying a tray near the window. For a moment, I felt terrible. Then an older customer came back through the door. She explained that the key had fallen into her bag while she was looking at the photos. She returned it immediately and apologized politely. Nobody did anything wrong, but I learned to check the wall more carefully after a busy day.

## 18. The Community Map

**Foco A2:** Comparatives and superlatives — review in context  
**Gancho:** “The smallest idea has become our biggest wall.”

This week, we made a community map for the café. At first, it was smaller than a poster, but people kept adding notes about their favorite places. Now it is the biggest thing on the wall. One student wrote about the quietest street in the neighborhood, and a taxi driver added the best place to watch the sunset. The map is more colorful than I imagined, and it is becoming more useful every day. I think the simplest ideas are sometimes the most powerful because everyone can add something.

## 19. A New Saturday Plan

**Foco A2:** *going to*, *will* and First Conditional — plans and decisions  
**Gancho:** “Next Saturday could change the whole project.”

Next Saturday, we are going to hold our first community morning. We are going to invite local artists, readers, and small food businesses. I will make a sign for the library, and Leo will create a playlist for the event. If the weather is good, we will put tables outside. If it rains, we will use the back room for the art activities. I know it will be a busy day, but I think people will come. If the event goes well, we are going to repeat it every month.

## 20. The Door Stays Open

**Foco A2:** Present Perfect, modals and infinitives — season finale  
**Gancho:** “Three weekends were supposed to be enough.”

Three weekends have passed since I opened The Corner Project, and the owner has asked me an important question: do I want to stay? I have thought about it every day. We must find a better way to manage the money, and I have to learn more about running a small business. But I still love meeting people and hearing their stories. I want to keep the café open, and I want to invite more volunteers to help. If we work together, this little corner will become more than a café. It will become a real community project.

## Puente hacia B1

La temporada termina con el café abierto, pero no resuelve todo: Maya todavía debe
atraer clientes, gestionar dinero, trabajar con voluntarios y decidir si el proyecto
puede continuar. B1 puede retomar la historia con opiniones, experiencias más
matizadas, problemas comunitarios, reseñas, decisiones complejas y un proyecto de
expansión.
